FuelWatch JA

Jamaica's Crowdsourced Fuel Price Tracker

Last updated: October 6, 2026

FuelWatch JA ("we", "our", or "us") is committed to protecting your privacy. This Privacy Policy explains how we collect, use, and safeguard your information when you use the FuelWatch JA mobile application.

Information We Collect

  • Account information: When you register, we collect your email address, username and parish.
  • Location data: We request your location to show nearby fuel stations and to verify that price reports are submitted from the correct location. Location is only accessed while the app is in use.
  • Price reports: When you submit a fuel price report, we store the price data, station details, timestamp, and optionally a photo of the price board.
  • Station ratings: When you rate a station for customer service or fuel or charger quality, we store your rating with your account.
  • Fuel Log: If you use My Fuel Log, we store the fill-ups you enter: date, station, fuel type, litres, price per litre, amount spent, and optionally your odometer reading and notes. You may also save your vehicle's tank size.
  • Profile information: You may optionally upload a profile photo.
  • Reports you send us: Bug reports, station flags and support messages, including your device type and operating system for bug reports.
  • Device information: We collect push notification tokens to send you price change alerts for your saved stations.

How We Use Your Information

  • To display crowdsourced fuel prices across Jamaica
  • To verify the accuracy of price reports
  • To send push notifications about price changes at your favourite stations
  • To calculate leaderboard rankings based on your contributions
  • To show each station's average rating to all users
  • To show you your own fuel spending, fuel economy and station comparisons in your Fuel Log
  • To respond to bug reports, station flags and support messages
  • To improve the accuracy and reliability of the app

Fuel Log and Station Ratings

Your Fuel Log is private to your account. It is never shown to other users, never shared with anyone, and only used to show you your own spending, fuel economy and which stations give you the best value. You can edit or delete any entry at any time.

Station ratings are stored with your account so you can change them later. Other users only see each station's average rating and the number of ratings, never who gave them.

Data Sharing

We do not sell your personal data. Your username and price contributions are visible to other users of the app as part of the crowdsourced reporting feature. Your email address is never shared publicly. Your Fuel Log is never visible to other users, and your station ratings are only shown as part of an average.

We use the following third-party services:

  • Supabase: database and authentication
  • Google Maps: map display and location services
  • Google Cloud Vision: reading prices from price board photos you submit
  • Expo / EAS: app delivery, updates and push notifications
  • Resend: transactional email delivery

Location Data

FuelWatch JA requests access to your device location while the app is in use. This is used to show nearby stations, filter results by distance, suggest nearby stations when you log a fill-up, and verify that price reports and verifications are submitted from within 125 metres of the station. We do not track your location in the background.

Photos

Photos submitted as proof of price reports are stored securely and used solely for moderation and for reading the prices shown. Profile photos are stored and displayed within the app.

Data Retention

Your account data is retained for as long as your account is active. Price reports and history are retained indefinitely to power historical charts. Fuel Log entries are kept until you delete them or your account.

You can delete your account at any time from the Settings screen in the app, or by contacting us. Deleting your account permanently deletes your account information, Fuel Log and station ratings.

Children's Privacy

FuelWatch JA is not directed at children under the age of 13. We do not knowingly collect personal information from children.

Security

We use industry-standard security measures including encrypted connections and secure cloud storage to protect your data. However, no method of transmission over the internet is 100% secure.

Your Rights

You have the right to access, correct, or request deletion of your personal data. You can edit or delete your Fuel Log entries and change your station ratings in the app at any time. To exercise any other rights, contact us at the email below.

Changes to This Policy

We may update this Privacy Policy from time to time. We will notify users of significant changes through the app. Continued use of the app after changes constitutes acceptance of the updated policy.
